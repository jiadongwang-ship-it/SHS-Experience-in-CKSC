My SHS Experience in CKSC

Summary:
My first four months in CKSC SHS were filled with challenges, responsibilities, and meaningful growth. Through events, lectures, teamwork, and communication, I learned important real-life skills that will guide me in college and my future career.

For the past 4 months of student life, I felt a lot of stress and hardship and had a hard time coping with it. However, these experiences also taught me important lessons that will help me when I attend college and when I start working.

  1. Events and Program Preparation:
    • Effective communication is the key to success.
    • Cooperation among classmates and teachers is a must.
    • Time management is essential to achieving our goals.
    • Ideas and suggestions should be taken seriously, but not to the point that they conflict with the general process. (span kept)
    • Choose someone capable to lead to avoid confusion. (span kept)
    • Peaceful communication is important, but assertiveness may be needed when others refuse to communicate respectfully. (span kept)
  2. Lecture:
    • Always be prepared for unexpected situations.
    • SHS life is more demanding than JHS: more assignments, tougher lectures, and more presentations.
    • Start thinking more maturely.
    • Adjusting to different teaching styles may take time.
    • Never be afraid to ask questions!
